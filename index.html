Who We Are

Beyond the
Spectacle

We are a collective of activists, artists and documentarians committed to safeguarding the Circassian existence. We believe that memory is political, images are evidence, and every protest is a performance.

Founded in response to the erasure of public space, we archive what institutions would rather forget, and amplify voices that power tries to silence.

Our work spans photography, digital art, publishing on social media, community and live events — always rooted in solidarity, never in spectacle.
